# Federal boundary data credits

The committed `watersheds.geojson` and `cities.geojson` contain generalized
extracts of United States federal government boundary data, in the public domain.
The application provides source credit and does not imply agency endorsement.

- USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset (WBD), HUC8 subbasins:
  https://www.usgs.gov/national-hydrography/watershed-boundary-dataset
  USGS copyright policy:
  https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits
- U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb, Incorporated Places:
  https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/4
  Census source documentation:
  https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html

See `manifest.json` for extraction URLs, dates, generalization and coverage.
These snapshots are for regional reference, not legal boundary determination.
